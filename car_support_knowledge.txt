1. APEX MOTORS CORPORATE OVERVIEW
Apex Motors is a certified regional partner of Suzuki vehicles, specializing in local distribution, customized spare parts logistics, and warranty claims management. Our headquarters is located in Warehouse B, Sector 4-A, Industrial Estate, Karachi.

2. SERVICE AND MAINTENANCE POLICIES
- Scheduled Maintenance: Every Suzuki vehicle serviced under Apex Motors must undergo routine inspection every 5,000 kilometers or 6 months, whichever comes first.
- Spark Plug Replacements: Platinum spark plugs (Part #SP-9921) must be replaced every 40,000 kilometers. Standard nickel spark plugs (Part #SP-1102) must be replaced every 15,000 kilometers.
- Engine Oil Specification: All Suzuki engines under warranty must use Apex-approved SAE 0W-20 fully synthetic engine oil. Use of non-approved lubricants voids the engine cylinder warranty automatically.

4. REFUND & RETURN POLICIES
- General Returns: Unused and undamaged spare parts in original packaging can be returned within 14 calendar days from the purchase date for a full refund. A 10% restocking fee applies to open-box returns.
- Electrical Components: Returns of electrical parts (such as Alternators, ECM units, and Spark plugs) are strictly prohibited unless the part is proven dead-on-arrival (DOA) by an authorized Apex technician within 48 hours of purchase.
- Special Orders: Custom ordered body parts or paint kits are non-refundable and require a non-refundable 40% deposit.

5. PAYMENT AND INVOICING RULES
- Deposit Policy: All parts orders exceeding a retail value of $500 require a minimum 30% upfront deposit before parts are dispatched from our warehouse.
- Corporate Accounts: Authorized dealerships have a net-30 credit payment term. Payments delayed beyond 30 days are subject to a 1.5% monthly compound interest penalty.
- Retail Purchases: We accept major credit cards, bank transfers, and secure mobile checkout. Cash-on-delivery (COD) is restricted to orders below $100.

6. WARRANTY CLAIMS PROCEDURES
- Warranty Registration: A Suzuki warranty claim is valid only if registered in the Apex portal within 10 days of vehicle delivery.
- Transmission Defect Claims: Any issues with the automatic CVT transmission unit must be diagnosed using the Apex OBD-II Scan tool. The resulting diagnostics log (log format .DAT) must be uploaded along with the claim form.
- Turnaround Time: Parts replacements under warranty are processed within 5 to 7 business days, depending on parts availability in Warehouse B.
